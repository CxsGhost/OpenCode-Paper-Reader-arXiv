\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This work examines how a full-duplex interaction model can move from managing conversation to carrying out tasks. Our analysis answers both parts of the question posed in Section~\ref{sec:intro}. In frontend--backend agents, task success is limited first by initiation: execution can be offloaded, but initiating a faithful request remains a frontend responsibility. We introduce Duopra, which learns to act through a unified interface for domain-tool calls and natural-language backend requests.

On FDB v3, Duopra recovers tasks left unexecuted by the evaluated baselines and improves multi-call completion beyond what more frequent invocation alone would explain. The resulting task agency coexists with improved performance on all three FDB v1.0 takeover rates, while projector-only repair mitigates speech degradation.
The loss of spontaneous backchanneling nevertheless shows that maintaining all conversational behaviors remains an open challenge.
