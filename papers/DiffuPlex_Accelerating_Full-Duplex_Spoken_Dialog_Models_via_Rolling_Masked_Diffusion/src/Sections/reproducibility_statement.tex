\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
We provide reproducibility information throughout the main paper and Appendices.
Section~\ref{sec:method} specifies the DiffuPlex training and inference procedures, including masked-future prediction, confidence-based retention, and user-aware revision, while Appendices provide implementation, data-processing, evaluation, ablation, and runtime-measurement details.
Table~\ref{atab:artifacts} lists the external datasets, pretrained models, evaluation tools, licenses, and public sources used in this work.
Selected generated audio and qualitative examples are available on the anonymous project page at
\url{https://diffuplex.github.io/}.
We plan to release the implementation and evaluation scripts after the review process.
